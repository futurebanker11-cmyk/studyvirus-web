## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Photos and Images:**
- We collect photographs and images of furniture pieces that you capture through the App's camera feature
- These images are processed by our AI analysis engine to identify furniture style, era, materials, and estimated market value
- Images are only retained for the duration of processing unless you choose to save them to your collection

**User Account Information (Optional):**
- If you create an account or sign up for Premium, we collect email address and account credentials
- Account information is used for authentication and Premium subscription management

**User-Generated Content:**
- Custom notes, collections, and boards you create within the App
- Style quiz responses to determine your design preferences
- Saved furniture identification history

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:**
- Device type, operating system, and version
- Unique device identifiers (IDFA/Advertising ID)
- Device model and manufacturer
- Storage and memory information

**Usage Data:**
- Features accessed within the App
- Furniture items identified or searched
- Time spent on the App and specific features
- Interactions with design styles, quizzes, and library content
- App crashes, errors, and technical performance data

**Location Information:**
- We do not collect precise GPS location data
- Approximate location may be inferred from device settings for app performance analytics only

**Camera Permissions:**
- Permission status for camera access (granted/denied)
- We only access your camera when you actively use the furniture scanning feature
